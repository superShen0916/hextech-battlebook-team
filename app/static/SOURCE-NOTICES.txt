Only exact IDs matched to entries present in kiwi.bin.json are used. Arena's same-name
effects are not used. Constants are substituted only from the exact KIWI RootSpell
when every level value is identical. Unresolved dynamic values remain explicitly marked;
live tooltip performance counters are omitted and are not claimed as measured match data.
The literal reference {{ Item_Keyword_OnHit }} resolves to the same-version string
item_keyword_onhit = On-Hit. It is displayed as 攻击特效（On-Hit）; 攻击特效 is also
used literally in the official Tencent KIWI dataset (augment 2016). {{SpellName}}
means the champion-dependent relevant ability and is displayed as 对应技能.
Effects are a 16.20 reference, not an exact reconstruction of another patch or champion state.
